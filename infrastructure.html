State-of-the-Art Infrastructure

Our college offers world-class infrastructure to facilitate the academic, cultural, and recreational growth of students. Explore the modern and well-equipped facilities available on campus:

Classrooms

Our classrooms are spacious, air-conditioned, and equipped with the latest audio-visual tools for an enhanced learning experience. Comfortable seating and ample space provide an ideal environment for studying.

Laboratories

The college houses well-equipped laboratories for various disciplines, including physics, chemistry, biology, and computer science. Students can access the latest equipment and resources for practical learning.

Sports Facilities

We provide an array of indoor and outdoor sports facilities, including basketball courts, football fields, badminton courts, and a gymnasium. Students are encouraged to maintain a healthy and active lifestyle.

Auditorium

The college auditorium is equipped with modern sound and lighting systems, providing a venue for cultural events, seminars, workshops, and presentations. It has a seating capacity of 500 people.

Library

Our library is a hub of knowledge with a vast collection of books, journals, e-books, and research papers. Students have access to both physical and digital resources for academic growth.

Hostels

The college offers separate hostels for male and female students with comfortable rooms, Wi-Fi, dining facilities, and 24/7 security. It is designed to provide a homely atmosphere for students away from home.
