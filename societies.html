Explore Our Societies and Clubs

Our college offers a variety of clubs and societies for students to join. Explore them below!

Drama Club

The Drama Club is a creative space for students interested in acting, directing, and stage production. The club hosts annual plays and performance arts events.

Music Society

The Music Society is for music enthusiasts who love to play instruments or sing. It organizes regular jam sessions, musical performances, and talent shows.

Photography Club

The Photography Club is for students who have an interest in photography. It organizes workshops, photography competitions, and exhibitions.

Sports Club

The Sports Club is for athletes and sports enthusiasts. It organizes inter-college sports events, competitions, and fitness programs.

Debate Society

The Debate Society fosters public speaking and critical thinking. It hosts regular debates and competitions for students to showcase their debating skills.

Volunteer Society

The Volunteer Society works towards social welfare. It organizes community outreach programs, charity events, and volunteering opportunities.
